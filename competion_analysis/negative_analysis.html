Winning Strategy

How to Prevail

01

Local-First Architecture

Reminders and schedules must be handled 100% on-device. Zero dependency on API connectivity for core utility.

02

High-Velocity UX

The "Log Dose" action should be the focal point of the app. Reduce friction. No mandatory sign-ups before the first log.

03

Privacy as a Premium

In a world of data sharing, "On-Device Only" is a powerful marketing tool. Market the lack of data-selling as a primary feature.

04

Robust Scheduling Engine

Support complex cycles: 21/7 day patterns, alternate days, and "X times per day" with adaptive rescheduling.
